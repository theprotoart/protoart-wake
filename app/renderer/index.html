PW-2 Operator's Card

ProtoArt Wake keeps your Mac from going to sleep for as long as you need, then lets it sleep normally again. It lives in the menu bar: click the tube to open this panel, right-click it for quick timers.

Power button

Press it to keep the Mac awake; press again to let it sleep. The tube glows while it's on. The menu-bar tube turns solid, and shows the time left when there's a timer.

Duration knob

  • ∞: until you switch it off.
  • 5 … 45 (minutes) and 1H … 12H (hours): a timer. Turning the knob during a session restarts the timer from now. An amber ring around the knob drains back toward ∞ as the time runs out.
  • SET: any length (like 1 h 30 min), a clock time ("until 6:30 PM"), while one app is running, or while a file downloads.

TIME LEFT counts down; AWAKE FOR shows how long it's been on. Under an hour they show minutes and seconds, above that hours and minutes (the small print under each says which). Turn the knob by grabbing its edge, scroll over it, or click a label. Double-click it for ∞.

Keep awake

  • DISPLAY + MAC: the screen stays on too.
  • MAC ONLY: the screen may go dark as usual, but the Mac keeps working: downloads, renders, uploads, backups.

Screen saver

BLOCK tells macOS you're still there, so the screen saver and the lock screen don't start (with DISPLAY + MAC). It needs no special permissions.

Auto

Lets the Mac stay awake by itself, even with the power button off:

  • APPS: while any app lit in the list is open. Click an app in the list to watch it (green = open now).
  • AUDIO: while anything plays or records sound, with a 30-second grace for gaps between songs or takes.
  • DOWNLOADS: while a browser download is in progress in your Downloads folder.
  • AT LOGIN: opens ProtoArt Wake when you log in (in standby).

If you switch it off while an Auto rule is holding the Mac awake, Auto waits until that reason goes away.

Lamps and status

  • HELD (green): macOS has confirmed the hold. It blinks while waiting for confirmation.
  • TIMER: a timer is running. AUTO: an Auto rule is on.
  • The amber window shows your Mac's normal sleep settings and which other apps are keeping it awake.

Menu bar

Right-click the tube for End and Extend Session, Indefinitely, Minutes, Hours, Other Time / Until, While App Is Running, While File Is Downloading, and Quick Settings. Press ⌃⌥⌘W anywhere to switch it on or off.

Set up

SETUP opens the Set-up Card: more triggers (a schedule, the power adapter, an external display, a drive, a busy processor), when sessions end by themselves (low battery, unplugging, sleep, switching user), reminders with Extend buttons, sounds, how the menu-bar tube looks and behaves, Drive Alive and statistics.

Pin

PIN keeps this panel open and floating on top. Drag it anywhere by the brand plate or the tube; it remembers where you put it. Unpin to go back to a drop-down under the menu-bar tube. Opening ProtoArt Wake from Applications shows this panel too.

Lights out · Quit

LIGHTS OUT turns the display off now (moving the mouse wakes it). QUIT closes the app and the Mac goes back to its normal sleep settings.

Good to know

  • If the app quits or crashes, macOS drops the hold immediately: your Mac can't get stuck awake.
  • A laptop still sleeps when you close the lid (unless an external display is connected), and ProtoArt Wake stops on its own at 10% battery (change it under SETUP).
  • Nothing is uploaded and the app makes no network connections.
